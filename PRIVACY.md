# Privacy Policy: Aetheria Clash

*Effective 2 October 2026*

Aetheria Clash does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any
personal information.

- **No accounts.** The game has no sign-in.
- **No network.** The game makes no network requests. There are no ads,
  analytics, crash reporters or third-party SDKs.
- **On your device only.** Your settings, win records, arcade progress and
  high scores are saved on your device with the system's standard storage.
  Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase them in Settings → Reset
  progress.
- **Game controllers and keyboards** are read only while you play and nothing
  about them is recorded.
- **Children.** Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from
  children.

Questions: use the support link on the game's App Store page.
